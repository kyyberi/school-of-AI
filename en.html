What is School4AI?

This is a community learning idea, not a commercial school, a large training program, or a finished course.

We are exploring practical activities for young people from around age 10: using AI, practicing English, working together, and creating something they can show to others.

Local input will help shape the initiative as it starts in Yên Bái and prepares for more locations later.

Practical value

What do young people get?

For young people from around age 10. The aim is not to sit through lectures. Young participants should leave with something real.

  • Practical AI skills for future studies and work
  • English practice in real situations
  • Experience using AI tools
  • Teamwork
  • Building something concrete
  • Presenting the result
  • Support from an experienced AI professional with a teaching background
  • A social and enjoyable learning experience
  • A School4AI participation certificate to take home
  • A small participation gift at the end

You do not need previous AI knowledge, computer experience, or coding knowledge. Activities can adapt to different ages and skill levels. Younger participants receive simpler and more guided challenges; older or more experienced participants may work more independently or take on more technical challenges.

I am interested

Not a lecture room

A small practical result matters more than a perfect lesson plan. The early activities are still taking shape, but the direction is clear: use AI, practice English, work with others, and make something visible. Where practical, teams should be formed around compatible age and skill levels.

Participation certificate

School4AI participation certificate

Each participant receives a School4AI participation certificate at the end of the activity.

  • Participation
  • Practical AI learning
  • English practice
  • Teamwork
  • Project work
  • Presentation of the result

The certificate is a participation certificate from the independent School4AI community initiative. It is not an official school qualification, academic credit, or government-recognized certificate.

For parents

Your child’s safety comes first.

School4AI activities will be designed for the age of the participants and held in an open, supervised environment.

Parents are welcome to stay, observe, and see what their children are doing.

Before a young person participates, we will ask for parent or guardian consent and collect the practical information needed to look after them during the activity.

  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Emergency contact information
  • Food allergies or other practical requirements
  • Permission for photographs
  • Age-appropriate grouping and activities
  • Clear information about the venue, schedule, and responsible adults

Young people will work in shared spaces and small teams. The intention is not to have private one-to-one sessions between an adult and a child.

We will also explain which AI tools are being used and what the young people are doing with them.

You are welcome to ask questions before deciding whether your child participates.

Parents are part of the community around School4AI.

Parents and other adults may also help with practical tasks such as:

  • Food
  • Water
  • Venue preparation
  • Registration
  • Cleanup
  • General support

This is an independent community activity and not part of the official school curriculum.

About the Igniter

About the Igniter

Dr. Jarkko Moilanen started the School4AI idea as a way to become more involved with the Vietnamese community before eventually settling permanently in Vietnam.

He is married to a Vietnamese woman and is still learning Vietnamese.

He works professionally with artificial intelligence in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, and visits Vietnam regularly.

His background also includes university studies in pedagogy in Finland, covering teaching methods and teaching skills.

He has previously organized dozens of Code School events in Finland, where young people learned technology through practical activities.

The same principle sits behind School4AI: learn by trying, building, working together, and sharing what you created.

Educational disclaimer

The activities are informal and practical. They are intended to complement, not replace, formal education.
