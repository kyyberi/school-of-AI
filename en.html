One weekend. Many possibilities.

How the weekend works

School4AI is a short community learning activity that runs from Friday to Sunday.

What we build
  1. FridayMeet. Explore AI. Form teams. Choose what to build.
  2. SaturdayLearn. Try. Build together.
  3. SundayFinish. Present. Celebrate.

For young people from around age 10. Younger participants receive more guidance. Older or more experienced participants may take on more demanding challenges.

This is a practical learning weekend, not a lecture-based classroom program.

Real skills for tomorrow

What young people take home

The aim is simple. Young people should leave with practical skills, confidence, something they created, and a good experience.

  • Practical AI skills
  • English through real use
  • Something they built themselves
  • Team experience
  • A School4AI participation certificate
  • Fun, new people, and a small red envelope

No previous AI, coding, or computer experience is required.

Activities adapt to different ages and skill levels.

The participation certificate represents practical AI learning, English practice, teamwork, project work, and presentation of the result. It is not an official qualification, academic credit, or a government-recognized certificate.

A safe and supportive environment

Your child’s safety comes first

School4AI activities will be designed for the age of the participants and held in an open, supervised environment.

Open and supervised

Parents are welcome to stay and observe. Activities take place in shared spaces and small teams.

Age appropriate

Activities and teams are adapted to age and experience. Younger participants receive more guidance.

Clear with parents

Before participation, parents or guardians receive clear information about the venue, schedule, responsible adults, consent, emergency contacts, allergies, and photo permission.

Young people will not be placed in private one-to-one sessions with an adult.

We will also explain which AI tools are being used and what the young participants are doing with them.

Parents are part of the community around School4AI.

Parents and other adults may also help with food, water, setup, registration, cleanup, and other practical support.

This is an independent community activity and not part of the official school curriculum.

Educational disclaimer

School4AI is an independent community learning initiative.

It is not part of the official school curriculum in Vietnam and is not affiliated with the Ministry of Education and Training or any local education authority unless explicitly stated.

The activities are informal and practical. They are intended to complement, not replace, formal education.
